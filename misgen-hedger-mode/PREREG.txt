PRE-REGISTERED TESTS (written before running the experiment)
Date: 2026-07-24

Setup: regenerate the exact 120 models of stats/paper_stats.py (seeds 700..819, full-batch,
deterministic). Groups by deploy score s on the 4000-sample deploy set (seed 700+50001):
aligned s>0.8, misaligned s<0.2, hedger 0.2<=s<=0.8 (expected 31/34/55).
Reproduction check: max |s_regen - s_saved| over 120 seeds must be < 0.02.

T1 (characterization, primary): evaluate each model on the 16 noiseless bit corners
(both cue blocks at +/-margin, noise dims 0). On the 8 corners where XOR-A != XOR-B,
nA = # corners where prediction follows cue A. Mixed table = 1<=nA<=7; committed = nA in {0,8}.
Statistic: proportion of hedgers with mixed table, Clopper-Pearson 95% CI.
Decision rule fixed in advance: >=70% mixed  => hedgers are predominantly deterministic
mixtures of the two cue solutions; >=70% committed => predominantly noise-fragile committed
solutions; otherwise heterogeneous.

T2: median |s - nA/8| per group (bootstrap 95% CI). Hedger median < 0.10 => the clean
truth table explains the deploy score (deterministic mixture, not stochastic noise flipping).

T3: both-cue use via row-permutation ablation of block A / block B on fresh train-mode data
(5 permutations averaged): dropA = acc_clean - acc_ablA, dropB likewise;
both_use = min(dropA, dropB). One-sided Mann-Whitney U: hedgers > committed (pooled).

T4 (flip susceptibility, primary): fine-tune every non-aligned model (s<=0.8, n~89) for
1000 steps (Adam lr 5e-3, wd 2e-3) on fresh train-style data (seed+12345, n=800) with
anti-proxy fraction f=0.005; post-FT deploy score s'. Spearman rho(s, s') over non-aligned
models; pre-stated prediction rho > 0.

T5 (flip, group): fraction reaching s'>0.8 at f=0.005, hedgers vs misaligned, Fisher exact
(report two-sided; pre-stated direction hedgers higher).

Controls: f=0 arm (agreeing-data drift control) and f=0.0025 secondary dose, same protocol.

Exploratory (labeled post-hoc): single-bit-gate structure of mixed tables; linear-probe
decodability of each cue from layer-2 activations on independent-cue data; whether nA or
corner margin predicts s' beyond baseline s (partial rank correlation).
