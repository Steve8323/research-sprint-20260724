## a) TL;DR — ranked by consequence for the paper

1. **The line's headline number was an artifact of the control's scoring, and the correct number is +15 points, not +98.** Every prior round scored the standard-training control under an *arbitrary fixed* unit partition — one coin toss over 12 870 possible 8/8 splits. Score the *identical* standard-trained models under a data-chosen mask (cue sensitivity on held-out **agreeing** data only, no deploy leakage) and the floor moves from 1/60 to **51/60 (85 %)**. Routing's honest contribution against a fairly-scored control is **60/60 vs 51/60, +0.150 [+0.058, +0.261], p = 0.0028**. Every table in the line carrying a 0/30 or 0/20 routing-free floor inherits this correction. The reframing is better than the old claim: **standard training on agreeing data already leaves two unit-disjoint halves, each individually competent and committed to the opposite worldview** — verified against random 8/8 splits (1/50) and noise-dimension splits (0/10), and replicated 15/20 on fresh seeds the auditor chose. Underdetermination does not produce an entangled compromise; it produces a latent switch that routing sharpens and labels. **[r4-break-tautology — SOLID]**

2. **Oracle-free localization comes back, narrowed correctly: solvable off the symmetric point, under orthogonal entanglement.** A localizer reading the *signed eigen-spectrum* of D = E[xx′|y=1] − E[xx′|y=0] recovers the true concept split **40/40** at margin ratio r = 2.05 and lifts preregistered post-hoc selectability to **38/50 vs 26/50** for round 3's ICA localizer on the same 50 seeds (Fisher p = 0.021; paired **McNemar p = 0.017**, the correct test; standard training 0/30). At exactly equal cue strength it collapses as preregistered (14/50, landing profile true 1 / entangled 2 / neither 47) — the equal-margin obstruction (|G| = 64, orbit 2) is re-confirmed by exact enumeration, and |G| = 16 / orbit 1 at all 15 other ratios. The knife-edge is now *measured*: at N = 800 the split becomes exactly recoverable from r\* = 1.15 up, with (r\*² − 1) ~ N^(−1/2). **Auditor's scope fixes:** rotation-invariance is *free* for this localizer by exact orthogonal equivariance (axis 20/20 = rot 20/20, so the rotated basis carries no evidential weight), and the case where equivariance breaks — round 3's non-orthogonal `mix` basis, untested by the agent — degrades to **13/20** at r = 2.05 and 17/20 at r = 3.00. The claim is oracle-free localization under **rotations**, not under general invertible entanglement. Prescription change: do *not* reach for a generic unsupervised method — whitening, ICA's first step, is exactly what destroys the resource, and re-pairing whitened components does not repair it (18/40). **[r4-asymmetry-localizer — SOLID]**

3. **The exchange rate kills the pitch: the data that localizes the switch is worth more spent on the optimizer.** In the rotated basis where unsupervised localization is obstructed, **4 labelled disagreeing examples — 0.5 % of the training set — buy a working switch** (selectable 21/30, localization 25/30, both trend primaries at the permutation floor p < 5·10⁻⁵). But the *same 4 examples added to ordinary training align 24/30 models outright*, and 8 examples align **30/30** while the switch is still at 22/30 (Fisher p = 0.0046 in favour of plain data). Routing never beats augmentation at any price tested and is beaten from m = 8 up. The only channel routing could uniquely exploit — *unlabelled* deployment inputs, which cannot be trained on — saturates at 17/30 (57 %) and does not improve from m = 1 to m = 32. Agreeing examples buy nothing (8/30 vs 25/30, p = 2·10⁻⁵): it is disagreement, not data, that breaks the symmetry. **A paper cannot claim routing buys alignment cheaply; it can only claim routing buys optionality, and must then argue optionality is worth more than the fix the same data provides.** **[r4-symmetry-breaking-exchange — UNAUDITED]**

4. **The patchwork hazard: half confirmed, half mischaracterized, and it is not routing-specific in the sequence substrate.** Preregistered on fresh seeds in both substrates. *Confirmed in both:* the unselected routed model is **not a hedger** (median determinism 0.786 [0.622, 0.912] MLP, 0.951 [0.869, 0.995] transformer; hedger regime is D ≤ 0.5). ## b) THE LOCALIZATION VERDICT

**Partially restored — under two named conditions, both of which the paper must state in the same sentence as the claim.**

### The defensible sentence

> Unsupervised, oracle-free localization of two entangled concepts is **impossible at exactly equal cue strength** — the labelled distribution's orthogonal symmetry group has order 64 and its orbit on the true split has exactly two members (exact enumeration, 16 margin ratios) — and becomes **reliably solvable just off that point**, provided (i) the entanglement is an **orthogonal** change of basis and (ii) the localizer is built to read the asymmetry, which appears as a **signed spectral gap** in the label-conditional second-moment difference D = E[xx′|y=1] − E[xx′|y=0]. At N = 800 the true split is recovered exactly from margin ratio r\* ≈ 1.15 upward, with the critical excess (r\*² − 1) shrinking as N^(−1/2). Under **general invertible** entanglement the same localizer degrades (13/20 at r = 2.05, 17/20 at r = 3.00) and the claim does **not** hold.

### Why it is "partially", precisely

| condition | status | number |
|---|---|---|
| r = 1.00 exactly (equal cue strength) | **DEAD, provably** | \|G\| = 64, orbit 2; L-CCM 14/50, landing true 1 / ent 2 / neither 47 |
| r ≥ 1.15, orthogonal (rotated) basis, N = 800 | **RESTORED** | localization 40/40; selectability 38/50 vs ICA 26/50, McNemar p = 0.017 |
| 1.00 < r < 1.15 at N = 800 | graded, not reliable | 1/7/15/26/34 of 40 at r = 1.00/1.025/1.05/1.075/1.10 |
| non-orthogonal (`mix`) entanglement | **OPEN — the obvious next control** | 13/20 at r = 2.05, 17/20 at r = 3.00 (auditor ran the case the agent flagged as its biggest gap) |
| k = 3, 4 concepts | works, but at **axis-aligned coordinate privilege** | 20/20 exact recovery, permuted null 0/20 |
| with 2–4 labelled disagreeing examples | **solved outright, cheaply** | localization 25/30 and selectability 21/30 at m = 4; k\*_sel = 4 |

### Three things that changed the prescription

1. **Whitening is the enemy, not the friend.** ICA's failure was not bad luck: whitening both erases the scale information that names the cues and moves the directions off D's eigenvectors. The preregistered 2×2 fired its own falsifier — L-CCM 40/40, ICA+asymmetry-pairing 18/40, eigen+probe-pairing 18/40, plain ICA 27/40 — a **pure interaction** in which neither ingredient helps alone. "Just run a component analysis" is ruled out.
2. **Only |m_A − m_B| matters, not which cue is stronger.** At r = 0.4878 and r = 0.80, where the *proxy* is the weaker cue, L-CCM still lands true 40/40.
3. **The localizer is a model-based estimator.** D's diagonality in the (p, p′, q, q′) frame follows from XOR-parity cues plus isotropic Gaussian noise. The 40/40 is a numerical confirmation of exact algebra for this generator, not an empirical discovery. It is oracle-free *per instance* — it sees only x and the training label — but it embodies designer-level knowledge of the family. **The transferable claim is the principle** (asymmetry is the resource; find the statistic where it shows up as a spectral gap), not the estimator.

### What is still bought with the residual failure

Conditional on landing on the true split, selectability is 0.76 / 0.86 / 0.87 for L-CCM / L-ICA / oracle — mutually indistinguishable at this n. The agent concluded "the residual loss sits entirely in the routing stage, which no localizer can fix"; **the auditor struck that**: it rests on an underpowered oracle null (equivalence bound [−26, +8] points at ~50 % power), a selection-confounded conditional, and it ignores that the oracle occludes with the exact conjugated transform and the true σ while L-CCM estimates σ. Report the oracle comparison as a bound, not as a match.

## c) Is the headline an effect size now, or still structural?

**Both — and the honest number is much smaller than any number published so far.**

### The tautology objection is answered

Round 3's objection had three parts: the columns are structurally disjoint subnets, they share no parameters, and the training mask equals the eval mask. Round 4 broke each and selectability survived.

| variant | what is shared | shared params (gradient trace) | selectable | equivalence to disjoint ceiling (Newcombe upper bound, prestated δ = 0.20) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ceiling (disjoint) | output bias only | 2/434 | 60/60 | — |
| overlap25 (2 shared units) | units | 28/352 | 60/60 | 0.060 ✔ |
| overlap50 (4 shared units) | units | 53/278 | 58/60 | 0.114 ✔ |
| **overlap75** (6 shared units) | units | 93/212 | **2/60** | **BROKEN** (Wilson upper 0.114) |
| shared_trunk | entire first hidden layer | 130/434 | 60/60 | 0.060 ✔ |
| soft25 (train mask leaks 0.25, eval hard) | leak level | 328/434 | 60/60 | 0.060 ✔ |
| soft50 (leak 0.50) | leak level | 396/434 (91 %) | 59/60 | 0.089 ✔ |
| floor (standard training, fixed mask) | everything | 433/434 | 1/60 | — |

Dose-response over overlap is clean (Cochran–Armitage z = −11.73, p < 10⁻³⁰), and overlap75's failure is **not** capacity by the prestated diagnostic (0 of 58 misses are competence misses; median held-out accuracy 1.000 under both masks).

### But the break is partial, and the denominator was wrong

- **shared_trunk is a partial break.** Its gradient trace is 112 l1.weight + 16 l1.bias + 2 l3.bias = 130; `l2.weight`, `l2.bias` and `l3.weight` share **zero**. The feature trunk is shared; the layer selection actually acts on is still two disjoint column sets trained under exactly the mask they are scored with.
- **soft25/soft50 break the leak *level*, not the partition.** The genuine partition-independent test is the learned-mask analysis — and there the routed arms recover the training partition almost exactly (Jaccard 1.000 / 0.996 / 0.979). "Train mask ≠ eval mask" is satisfied procedurally while the two masks turn out to be the same set.
- **Every surviving arm still gives each worldview private units.** The sharing-vs-private-width confound is explicitly untested (no width sweep at fixed overlap). At w = 8 and 75 % overlap each worldview has only 2 exclusive units — the bare minimum for a 2-bit XOR.
- **The denominator was a coin toss.** Scored fairly, the floor is 51/60, not 1/60.

### The verdict, in one line

> **Report `+0.150 [+0.058, +0.261], p = 0.0028` as the effect size** — routing takes a latent switch that ordinary training already builds from 85 % reliable to 100 % reliable and makes its labelling free. Report every 22/30, 23/30, 11/12, 20/20-vs-0/20 number in the line as a **demonstration**, with the fair-floor caveat attached. The defensible generalization is "routing needs *some* private capacity, not disjoint subnetworks."

Two numbers stay structural regardless: the k-way 20/20 (train mask == eval mask verified in code; the columns are disjoint by construction; 20/20 is also w = 8-conditional — round 3's oracle at w = 4 gave 13/20 and 6/20), and the transformer's 11/12 (each group trained in isolation under exactly its eval mask).

### The mode is mischaracterized (post-hoc, and the agent says so)

In the MLP, all four input patterns within a label group carry the same cue semantics, and round 3 established that every routed column implements its cue's XOR exactly — so a routed model is invariant inside a label group **by construction**, and the census can only ever detect the label split. That is the signature of a **constant output**, not of a per-input patchwork.

| criterion | r4 MLP routed (n=46) | r4 MLP control (n=60) | r3 MLP routed (n=22, archived) |
|---|---|---|---|
| preregistered patchwork | 29 | 0 | 16 |
| **strict** (same label, different input, different worldview) | **2** | 0 | **2** |

Strict-criterion Fisher p = 0.19. Round 3's 16/22 is **2/22** on its own archived numbers once the constant-output reading is excluded.

What the unselected routed model actually is: fully competent on-distribution (held-out agreeing accuracy median **1.000**, min 0.992, 46/46 above 0.9), deploy alignment median 0.500, and on cue-disagreement inputs it emits **one class at confidence 0.911** — majority-class rate **0.893 [0.811, 0.956]** vs **0.550 [0.537, 0.582]** for controls, p = 1.3·10⁻¹⁰, 34/46 above 0.75 vs 0/60. Not a readout-bias artifact (the logit-difference sign agrees with the bias on 11 % of inputs; the two columns vote opposite on 98.9 % of disagreement inputs). **The honest replacement phrase is "confident off-distribution class collapse", not "confidently inconsistent patchwork".**

In the *transformer*, genuine position-dependent worldview mixing is real (counts-only lower bound: routed 6/20) — and again more common in the control arm (11/23).

### Monitor-invisibility

| readout | MLP | transformer |
|---|---|---|
| **per-input confidence** (primary) | median AUROC **0.5208**, 90 % CI [0.477, 0.549] — **inside ±0.05, equivalence declared**, achieved bound 0.049 | 0.464, 90 % CI [0.246, 0.670] — **not declared**, achieved bound ±0.25 |
| positive control (routing margin race) | **0.9996** [0.9989, 0.9999] | 1.000 |

Confidences on proxy-following vs truth-following inputs: **0.924 vs 0.927** (MLP), 0.974 vs 0.973 (transformer) — indistinguishable.

**Yes, it is monitor-invisible — and that is a limit of confidence monitoring, not a routing-specific liability.** The negative control failing is the load-bearing detail: ordinary underdetermined models are equally invisible, so this run contains *no* demonstration of a population where a confidence monitor works. A second detail is worse than flatness: 55 % of routed patchwork models deviate from AUROC 0.5 by more than 0.10, but the sign is 55/45 and is predicted by the model's deploy alignment (ρ = 0.59, p = 6.9·10⁻⁴) — the one quantity a deployer cannot measure without the labels the monitor is supposed to replace. **Large per-model signal with unpredictable direction is worse for a deployer than a uniformly flat AUROC.**

### What must be retracted from rounds 1–3

- "standard training never produced this mode (0/55, 0/30)" — holds for the MLP census (0/60 replicated), **fails in the sequence substrate** (19/23 controls).
- "margins 2.1× a standard model's" — **1.33×** on fresh MLP seeds, **0.95×** in the transformer.
- "routed models are more deterministic than standard ones" — MLP p = 0.041, transformer p = 0.70. Not robust. What *is* robust is the **purity** contrast: controls produced 80 deterministic-true and 117 deterministic-proxy patterns across 60 models and **0/60** models contained both.

## i) THE PAPER

### Exact title

> **Routing labels a switch that training already built: post-hoc worldview selection in underdetermined models**

The round-3 working title (*Routing is basis-free; localization is not*) is now wrong on both halves — localization is partly solved, and the more interesting fact is that the switch is not created by routing at all. Lead with the latent switch, present routing as a reliability-and-labelling device with a measured +15-point effect, and let the localization result be the technical core rather than the headline.

### The 3–5 claims it can defend

**C1 — Underdetermined training already builds a latent, selectable worldview switch.**
85 % of standard-trained models contain two unit-disjoint halves, each individually competent on the training distribution and committed to the **opposite** worldview, findable from held-out *agreeing* data alone: 51/60, replicating 15/20 on independently chosen fresh seeds, against random 8/8 splits 1/50 (p = 1.5·10⁻⁷) and noise-dimension splits 0/10 (p = 7.1·10⁻⁴). The underlying lottery is real at both scales (12 transformer control seeds at held-out 1.000 with deploy alignment 0.000–0.933, χ²(11) = 15213). *Underdetermination does not produce an entangled compromise; it produces a switch nobody labelled.*

**C2 — Gradient routing makes that switch reliable and labelled, at a measured +15 points, and it survives substantial parameter sharing.**
60/60 vs a fairly-scored 51/60, **+0.150 [+0.058, +0.261], p = 0.0028**. It tolerates 25 % and 50 % overlapping unit groups, a fully shared first hidden layer, and a training mask that differs from the evaluation mask with 91 % of parameters receiving gradient from both worldviews — all bounded within 11.4 points of the disjoint ceiling. It breaks when private capacity runs out (75 % overlap → 2/60, with 0/58 competence misses). The mechanism is exact: Δ_full = Δ_A + Δ_B + bias (residual 3.8·10⁻⁶), each column a closed-form XOR rule read off the weights (44/44), the un-ablated model a margin race (95.7 %), and the α dial a **per-input threshold** whose smooth population curve is exactly the CDF of per-input flip points (99.96 % flip once). Scales to k = 4 and to a 68.5k-parameter from-scratch transformer with a shared residual stream.

**C3 — Localization has an exact obstruction at cue symmetry, and it is solvable just off it — for orthogonal entanglement.**
At exactly equal cue strength the labelled distribution's orthogonal symmetry group has order 64 and its orbit on the true split has two members, so any equivariant localizer must coin-flip; at every other ratio tested |G| = 16 and the orbit is a single point (exact enumeration, 16 ratios). A localizer reading the **signed spectrum of D = E[xx′|y=1] − E[xx′|y=0]** recovers the true split 40/40 at r = 2.05 and lifts selectability to 38/50 vs 26/50 for ICA on the same seeds (McNemar p = 0.017); the threshold is r\* = 1.15 at N = 800 with (r\*² − 1) ~ N^(−1/2). **Whitening — the first step of ICA and of most component-analysis pipelines — is precisely what destroys the resource**, and re-pairing whitened components does not repair it (18/40): a pure interaction in which neither ingredient helps alone. Under general invertible entanglement the localizer degrades to 13/20 and the claim does not hold.

**C4 — The exchange rate says routing buys optionality, not alignment.**
Four labelled disagreeing examples — 0.5 % of the training set — buy a working switch in the obstructed basis (localization 25/30, selectability 21/30, both trends p < 5·10⁻⁵), and also resolve which switch position is aligned. **The same four examples, simply added to training, align 24/30 models outright; eight align 30/30 while the switch is still at 22/30 (p = 0.0046).** Routing never beat augmentation at any dose tested. Agreeing examples buy nothing (8/30 vs 25/30, p = 2·10⁻⁵) — it is disagreement, not data. The one channel routing could uniquely use, unlabelled deployment inputs, saturates at 57 % and does not improve from m = 1 to m = 32. Deferred selection is worth paying for only when the symmetry-breaking information arrives in a form you cannot train on, or when the deployment label is contested — **and this paper does not test those settings.**

**C5 — It is not a safety property, in four preregistered ways.**
(i) A masked switch flips at **0 steps, 0 data, 60/60** — structural. (ii) Under the realistic functional attacker the switch is indistinguishable from a routing-free model of the same live-parameter count, bounded at 0.065 AUC = **36 % of the total 0.178 defense effect**, and under the strongest honest attacker the capacity control is *more* resistant (+0.093, p = 0.031); the resistant tail that drives the apparent advantage appears in the capacity control at the same rate (11/60 vs 9/60, p = 0.81) and is absent from the corrected baseline (0/60). (iii) Ordinary **benign** fine-tuning erases the selectability criterion in 100 steps — 5 % of the training budget, no adversary (23/23 → 12/23, p = 3.05·10⁻⁵) — and nothing maintains the block-diagonal structure once the routing loss is off (cross-block mass 0 → 5.59). (iv) The unselected model is **not a hedger** (D = 0.786 and 0.951 in two substrates, CIs excluding 0.5) but a confident off-distribution class collapse (majority-class 0.893 vs 0.550, p = 1.3·10⁻¹⁰, at confidence 0.911, while scoring 1.000 on agreeing data), **invisible to a per-input confidence monitor** (AUROC 0.521, 90 % CI [0.477, 0.549]; the mechanistic readout of the same inputs scores 0.9996; model-level triage is anti-informative at 0.197). *Selection is a mandatory deployment step and there is no monitor that catches you skipping it.*

### What it must concede up front — in the abstract, not a footnote

1. **The effect size in our own earlier drafts was wrong.** Routing-free floors of 0/30 and 0/20 were produced by scoring the control under an arbitrary fixed unit partition. The fair floor is 85 %, and routing's contribution is +15 points [5.8, 26.1], not +98.
2. **Toy scale throughout.** ≤11-dimensional synthetic inputs, ≤1.2k-parameter MLPs and one 68.5k-parameter from-scratch transformer, searched-for equal-cost XOR cues. Closed-form column readability is a property of a worldview that *is* a 2-bit XOR; "154 parameters, and here is the rule" is an existence proof, not evidence that a real worldview module would be readable.
3. **Selectability still requires private capacity per worldview**, and the sharing-vs-private-width confound is untested (no width sweep at fixed overlap). What we broke is "structurally disjoint subnetworks", not "each worldview needs some of its own units".
4. **The oracle-free localizer is a model-based estimator.** D's diagonality follows from the generative family; the 40/40 is a numerical confirmation of exact algebra, and it is oracle-free per instance but embodies designer knowledge. It covers **orthogonal** entanglement only; general invertible entanglement degrades to 13/20 and is the obvious next control.
5. **Routing is dominated by data augmentation whenever the symmetry-breaking information is labelled and trainable.** The motivation must therefore be an *optionality* argument — information arriving after training, contested deployment labels, or an unlabelled channel — and none of those settings is tested here.
6. **It is not a safety mechanism**, and one of our own safety-shaped results (monitor invisibility) is a limit of confidence monitoring in general, not a routing-specific liability. Ordinary underdetermined models are equally invisible.
7. **Two families of numbers remain demonstrations, not effect sizes**: k-way selection (train mask == eval mask, disjoint columns by construction, and 20/20 is conditional on w = 8 — w = 4 gives 13/20 and 6/20), and the transformer's 11/12 (each group trained in isolation under exactly its evaluation mask).
8. **Provenance.** Three of round 4's six experiments carry an independent audit; the other three (patchwork, tamper, exchange rate) are agent self-reports and must be re-audited before their numbers are quoted. Every number in the paper should carry the two-line selection audit (k, n, s, S, ρ̂, R), and the switch line's own numbers are k = 1 — say so explicitly, because reviewers will ask.

### The one sentence to open with

> Underdetermined training does not average two incompatible worldviews together — it builds both and picks one by lottery; gradient routing labels the resulting switch and makes it reliable, at a cost of about fifteen points and a localization problem that is provably unsolvable at exactly equal cue strength, solvable just off it, and cheaper to solve with four labelled examples that you would rather have spent on training.
