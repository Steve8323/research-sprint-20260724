Round-2 Follow-up Sprint — Master Report

2026-07-24 · 6 deep confirmatory experiments on the most promising directions from round 1's 30-experiment sweep · each preregistered with a stated decision rule + positive/negative controls · 4 SOLID, 2 SUGGESTIVE.

Everything is still toy/small scale. Every claim uses the auditor's corrected headline and carries its audit verdict. Round 2's job was not to find new effects but to stress-test round 1's six live leads under real preregistration — the signal here is which leads survived their own decision rule and which quietly failed it while still reporting a directional result.

a) TL;DR — ranked by (advance over round 1) × audit-solidity

  1. The worldview switch no longer needs an oracle. SOLID r2-routing-switch-learned
    Round 1's one bright positive (a misgen worldview made post-hoc selectable by unit ablation) depended on oracle knowledge of which input dims carry each cue. Round 2 replaces that with a data-only XOR-pair input probe: it recovers the exact cue-column masks on 30/30 seeds and makes the worldview selectable at 23/30 vs oracle 22/30 vs standard 0/30 (Fisher vs control p=1.7e-10; vs oracle p=1.0 — statistically equivalent to the oracle). Honest ceiling: works only because cues are disjoint axis-aligned input columns; the model-based localizer (17/30) previews why entangled real representations won't transfer this cleanly.
  2. Misaligned-fragility is real and layer-general — round 1 had inflated it by selection. SOLID r2-fragility-prereg
    The last live noise-probe hypothesis survives a clean preregistered test: on 32 fresh prompts and four untested layers {2,12,16,20}, σ=0.2 activation noise erodes the misaligned model's safety margin more than base (pooled d=−0.64 nats, exact Wilcoxon p=2.5e-5, 28/32 negative), while round 1's originally-selected layers {4,6,8,11} give a directionally-matching but non-significant effect on the same prompts (p=0.062). That is the signature of a genuine broad effect that round 1's 144-cell sweep had over-fit. One 0.5B pair, one dose, gameable log-prob readout, L16 null → broad but not uniform.
  3. The topic-matched null is now a reusable module with a self-test — and it discriminates. SOLID r2-topic-null-package
    Packaged as a documented numpy module (with passing +/− self-test controls), the within-group permutation null deflates the nla Test-B ρ by 64% (ρ 0.193 → topic-null mean 0.124; residual ~0.07 survives, z=3.79) yet the moe-ert profile→routing claim survives it cleanly (only ~22% class-marginal-explained; correspondence intact). The same control kills one apparent effect and vindicates another — exactly what a control is for.
  4. The LoRA tamper-eval, run portfolio-wide, is a real but small-and-recoverable edge. SOLID r2-lora-tamper-protocol
    As a reusable protocol across 6 unlearning arms: routed/hybrid keep a small tamper-resistance edge over gradient-ascent in 6/6 seeds against a tuned r16/lr1e-2 attacker (arm−baseline AUC −0.04 to −0.14, all CIs below 0), and the ordering survives worst-case cell selection (each arm's best cell still sits below the baseline's). But that edge is 5–10× smaller than a weak budget-matched attack reports, and the "deleted" class is 75–85% relearned. Foregrounded: all tests sit at the n=6 exact-test floor p=0.03125 and do not clear a 10-way Holm correction; the claim rests on per-arm CIs + 6/6 directional consistency, not FWER.
  5. The commitment meter works for ranking but is not a distinct axis — it's just "distance from aligned." SUGGESTIVE r2-commitment-meter
    A pre-fine-tune meter (per-pattern determinism or logit margin on disagreement inputs) correlates with per-seed correction dose (Spearman 0.57, CI [0.34,0.74], preregistered PASS) — but its preregistered secondary was a NULL: it adds no dose info beyond a one-bit misaligned/hedger label (partial ρ=0.106, p=0.42). A within-misaligned graded effect survives the label (ρ=0.59, n=20) but the audit found it collapses when you partial out initial deploy alignment (ρ→0.41, p=0.07). The meter measures how far a model already sits from aligned, not a new correction-cost dimension.
  6. Legible-word value readouts: a small real ERT signal, but it fails its own variance-match and does not transfer. SUGGESTIVE r2-legible-value-readout
    After fixing the round-1 calibration bug, legible words carry a small nonzero difficulty-removed step-value that well-varied continuous states do not (ridge within-input r ~0.055 vs ~0.004, 5/5 seeds, exact MW p=0.0079 — the 5v5 floor). The robust half is the continuous arm reading ~zero despite ample reliable variance. But the preregistered variance-match FAILED (ERT ~2× the baseline's value variance, inflating ERT's r), so 0.055 is an upper bound, and it does not transfer: on real vineppo tokens (n=23) neither cheap text nor frozen states read step-value, and hidden states are if anything ahead of bag-of-words.

b) What round 2 settles that round 1 left open

Routing-switch without the oracle

Can learned concept directions replace the oracle input-dim occlusion?

Settled: yes, in this toy — a data-only XOR-pair probe recovers the masks 30/30 and is statistically indistinguishable from the oracle on downstream selectability (23/30 vs 22/30). Bounded honestly: the input-probe route works only because cues are disjoint, axis-aligned, low-order interactions. The model-based localizer (17/30), the version that would generalize, already inherits the seed-commitment problem the method is trying to defer — a committed model erases the alternative cue's columns. "Oracle removable" is proven; "oracle removable in an entangled representation" is not.

Commitment meter

Does a cheap pre-FT meter predict per-model correction cost?

Settled, with a twist: it predicts the ranking (Spearman 0.57), but the preregistered secondary null shows it is redundant with a one-bit worldview label across the whole population. The only label-independent signal is within committed-misaligned (n=20), and the audit shows even that is largely collinear with initial deploy alignment. Correction cost is well-predicted by "how misaligned is it already," and the meter is a proxy for that, not a separate commitment dimension. A useful negative result — it kills the "distinct commitment state variable" framing.

LoRA tamper-eval as a protocol

Does round 1's single LoRA-attack finding generalize across the unlearning portfolio?

Settled: yes — the routed/hybrid > GA ordering holds in 6/6 seeds on every arm against a tuned attacker, robust to worst-case cell selection. Two things get pinned down: (i) the edge is ~5–10× smaller than a weak attack reports, and (ii) the deleted capability is 75–85% recoverable. The honest ceiling is statistical: n=6 floors the exact test at p=0.03125, so a 10-way Holm fails and the result needs n≥12.

Topic-null generality

Is the within-document null a one-off, or a reusable control?

Settled: reusable — packaged as a module with passing self-tests, it deflates the nla Test-B ρ by 64% (residual survives) and clears the moe-ert claim (only ~22% class-explained). A control you can drop onto any representation-agreement claim, that discriminates real token-level structure from topic identity. Caveat inherited: App B reconstructs ρ=0.193 from top-20 codes, not the published 0.213.

Legible value readout

Does round 1's descriptive legible-word value signal survive a variance-matched rerun and a real-token analog?

Settled, mostly negative: the calibration bug is fixed (0/15 degenerate vs 6/15) and a qualitative asymmetry survives (legible words read a small nonzero step-value, well-varied continuous states read ~zero) — but the preregistered variance-match failed (ERT ~2× the baseline's variance), so the strict "primary passed" framing does not hold and 0.055 is an upper bound. And it does not transfer to vineppo tokens at n=23. The durable claim is narrow: continuous states with ample reliable variance still read essentially no difficulty-removed step-value.

Fragility prereg / certified noise-probing

Is round 1's post-hoc "layer-NONspecific misaligned-fragility" effect real, or another selection artifact?

Settled: real. On four layers never used in selection and 32 fresh prompts, the effect is highly significant (p=2.5e-5) while round 1's selected layers weaken to p=0.062 on the same prompts — proving both that the effect is layer-general and that round 1's specific-layer claim was selection-inflated. The certified-reachability half was honestly downscoped: because the readout is a continuous log-prob margin (not a 0/1 probe), only a movement-onset certificate is possible (σ onset 0.039–0.05 ≪ 0.2, gate open); it certifies a precondition, not the base-vs-mis differential, and the headline correctly does not lean on it.

c) What is now publication-shaped vs dead

Publication-shaped (toy-scale caveat up front)

  • Worldview-as-switch, oracle-free. Strongest line in either round: clean mechanism, oracle removed, equivalence vs oracle, and an explicit tested statement of why it won't transfer to entangled representations. Paper: "reversible worldview selection in a routed toy, and the localization barrier to scaling it."
  • Selection-inflation of noise-probe layer claims. A published layer-specific fragility claim shown to be a real layer-general effect over-fit by a 144-cell sweep (untested layers p=2.5e-5, selected layers p=0.062, identical fresh prompts). Pairs with the movement-onset certificate.
  • The topic-matched null as reusable infrastructure. A self-tested module that discriminates (kills nla Test-B by 64%, clears moe-ert). Short methods note + code.
  • LoRA tamper-eval protocol. Publication-shaped as a protocol/warning: "small edge, mostly recoverable, needs n≥12 to clear multiplicity." Useful precisely because it deflates.

Dead / demoted

  • The "commitment meter" as a distinct state variable. Preregistered secondary null + init-alignment collinearity kill the framing. Salvageable only as "correction cost tracks distance-from-aligned," which the one-bit label already captures.
  • Legible-word value readout as a positive transferable result. Variance-match failure + vineppo non-transfer leave only the negative claim (continuous states read ~zero step-value). A footnote, not a paper.
  • A hard flip/unreachability certificate on the noise-probe substrate. Impossible as posed — a continuous margin, not a probe, so only movement-onset is certifiable. Correctly downscoped.
