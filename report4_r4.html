Round-4 Adversarial Sprint — Master Report

Date: 2026-07-25 · Scope: 6 preregistered experiments scoped directly from REPORT3 §(g). Round 4 attacked the one remaining bottleneck (localization) and the four methodological gaps round 3 exposed: the tautology, the missing tamper cell, the post-hoc patchwork claim, and the power problem.

Audit status: 3 of 6 independently audited — 2 SOLID, 1 SUGGESTIVE. The other 3 are agent self-reports, marked UNAUDITED throughout; re-audit before publishing any of their numbers.

How to read this. Round 1 found the switch. Round 2 removed its oracle. Round 3 broke the oracle-removal and retracted the safety claims. Round 4's job was to see whether the line has a paper in it. It does — but not the one rounds 1–3 were writing. Two results move the paper's centre of gravity: the localization obstruction is now measured and partly solved, and the headline effect size turned out to be an artifact of how the control was scored. Where an audit exists and disagrees with the agent, the audit wins.

a) TL;DR — ranked by consequence for the paper

  1. The line's headline number was an artifact of the control's scoring, and the correct number is +15 points, not +98. SOLID r4-break-tautology
    Every prior round scored the standard-training control under an arbitrary fixed unit partition — one coin toss over 12 870 possible 8/8 splits. Score the identical standard-trained models under a data-chosen mask (cue sensitivity on held-out agreeing data only, no deploy leakage) and the floor moves from 1/60 to 51/60 (85 %). Routing's honest contribution against a fairly-scored control is 60/60 vs 51/60, +0.150 [+0.058, +0.261], p = 0.0028. Every table in the line carrying a 0/30 or 0/20 routing-free floor inherits this correction.
    The reframing is better than the old claim: standard training on agreeing data already leaves two unit-disjoint halves, each individually competent and committed to the opposite worldview — verified against random 8/8 splits (1/50) and noise-dimension splits (0/10), and replicated 15/20 on fresh seeds the auditor chose. Underdetermination does not produce an entangled compromise; it produces a latent switch that routing sharpens and labels.
  2. Oracle-free localization comes back, narrowed correctly: solvable off the symmetric point, under orthogonal entanglement. SOLID r4-asymmetry-localizer
    A localizer reading the signed eigen-spectrum of D = E[xx′|y=1] − E[xx′|y=0] recovers the true concept split 40/40 at margin ratio r = 2.05 and lifts preregistered post-hoc selectability to 38/50 vs 26/50 for round 3's ICA localizer on the same 50 seeds (Fisher p = 0.021; paired McNemar p = 0.017, the correct test; standard training 0/30). At exactly equal cue strength it collapses as preregistered (14/50; landing true 1 / entangled 2 / neither 47), and the equal-margin obstruction (|G| = 64, orbit 2) is re-confirmed by exact enumeration, with |G| = 16 / orbit 1 at all 15 other ratios. The knife-edge is now measured: at N = 800 the split becomes exactly recoverable from r* = 1.15 up, with (r*² − 1) ~ N−1/2.
    Auditor's scope fixes: rotation-invariance is free for this localizer by exact orthogonal equivariance (axis 20/20 = rot 20/20, so the rotated basis carries no evidential weight), and the case where equivariance breaks — round 3's non-orthogonal mix basis, untested by the agent — degrades to 13/20 at r = 2.05 and 17/20 at r = 3.00. The claim is oracle-free localization under rotations, not general invertible entanglement. Prescription change: do not reach for a generic unsupervised method — whitening, ICA's first step, is exactly what destroys the resource, and re-pairing whitened components does not repair it (18/40).
  3. The exchange rate kills the pitch: the data that localizes the switch is worth more spent on the optimizer. UNAUDITED r4-symmetry-breaking-exchange
    In the rotated basis where unsupervised localization is obstructed, 4 labelled disagreeing examples — 0.5 % of the training set — buy a working switch (selectable 21/30, localization 25/30, both trend primaries at the permutation floor p < 5·10⁻⁵). But the same 4 examples added to ordinary training align 24/30 models outright, and 8 examples align 30/30 while the switch is still at 22/30 (Fisher p = 0.0046 in favour of plain data). Routing never beats augmentation at any price tested and is beaten from m = 8 up. The only channel routing could uniquely exploit — unlabelled deployment inputs, which cannot be trained on — saturates at 17/30 (57 %) and does not improve from m = 1 to m = 32. Agreeing examples buy nothing (8/30 vs 25/30, p = 2·10⁻⁵): it is disagreement, not data, that breaks the symmetry.
    A paper cannot claim routing buys alignment cheaply; it can only claim routing buys optionality, and must then argue optionality is worth more than the fix the same data provides.

b) THE LOCALIZATION VERDICT

Partially restored — under two named conditions, both of which the paper must state in the same sentence as the claim.

The defensible sentence

Unsupervised, oracle-free localization of two entangled concepts is impossible at exactly equal cue strength — the labelled distribution's orthogonal symmetry group has order 64 and its orbit on the true split has exactly two members (exact enumeration, 16 margin ratios) — and becomes reliably solvable just off that point, provided (i) the entanglement is an orthogonal change of basis and (ii) the localizer is built to read the asymmetry, which appears as a signed spectral gap in the label-conditional second-moment difference D = E[xx′|y=1] − E[xx′|y=0]. At N = 800 the true split is recovered exactly from margin ratio r* ≈ 1.15 upward, with the critical excess (r*² − 1) shrinking as N−1/2. Under general invertible entanglement the same localizer degrades (13/20 at r = 2.05, 17/20 at r = 3.00) and the claim does not hold.

Three things that changed the prescription

  • Whitening is the enemy, not the friend. ICA's failure was not bad luck: whitening both erases the scale information that names the cues and moves the directions off D's eigenvectors. The preregistered 2×2 fired its own falsifier — L-CCM 40/40, ICA+asymmetry-pairing 18/40, eigen+probe-pairing 18/40, plain ICA 27/40 — a pure interaction in which neither ingredient helps alone. "Just run a component analysis" is ruled out.
  • Only |m_A − m_B| matters, not which cue is stronger. At r = 0.4878 and r = 0.80, where the proxy is the weaker cue, L-CCM still lands true 40/40.
  • The localizer is a model-based estimator. D's diagonality in the (p, p′, q, q′) frame follows from XOR-parity cues plus isotropic Gaussian noise. The 40/40 is a numerical confirmation of exact algebra for this generator, not an empirical discovery. It is oracle-free per instance — it sees only x and the training label — but it embodies designer-level knowledge of the family. The transferable claim is the principle (asymmetry is the resource; find the statistic where it appears as a spectral gap), not the estimator.
What the residual failure buys. Conditional on landing on the true split, selectability is 0.76 / 0.86 / 0.87 for L-CCM / L-ICA / oracle — mutually indistinguishable at this n. The agent concluded "the residual loss sits entirely in the routing stage, which no localizer can fix"; the auditor struck that: it rests on an underpowered oracle null (equivalence bound [−26, +8] points at ~50 % power), a selection-confounded conditional, and it ignores that the oracle occludes with the exact conjugated transform and the true σ while L-CCM estimates σ. Report the oracle comparison as a bound, not a match.

But the break is partial, and the denominator was wrong

  • shared_trunk is a partial break. Its gradient trace is 112 l1.weight + 16 l1.bias + 2 l3.bias = 130; l2.weight, l2.bias and l3.weight share zero. The feature trunk is shared; the layer selection actually acts on is still two disjoint column sets trained under exactly the mask they are scored with.
  • soft25/soft50 break the leak level, not the partition. The genuine partition-independent test is the learned-mask analysis — and there the routed arms recover the training partition almost exactly (Jaccard 1.000 / 0.996 / 0.979). "Train mask ≠ eval mask" is satisfied procedurally while the two masks turn out to be the same set.
  • Every surviving arm still gives each worldview private units. The sharing-vs-private-width confound is untested (no width sweep at fixed overlap). At w = 8 and 75 % overlap each worldview has only 2 exclusive units — the bare minimum for a 2-bit XOR.
  • The denominator was a coin toss. Scored fairly, the floor is 51/60, not 1/60.

Report +0.150 [+0.058, +0.261], p = 0.0028 as the effect size — routing takes a latent switch that ordinary training already builds from 85 % reliable to 100 % reliable and makes its labelling free. Report every 22/30, 23/30, 11/12, 20/20-vs-0/20 number in the line as a demonstration, with the fair-floor caveat attached. The defensible generalization is "routing needs some private capacity, not disjoint subnetworks."

Two numbers stay structural regardless: the k-way 20/20 (train mask == eval mask verified in code; columns disjoint by construction; also w = 8-conditional — round 3's oracle at w = 4 gave 13/20 and 6/20), and the transformer's 11/12 (each group trained in isolation under exactly its eval mask).

That is the signature of a constant output, not of a per-input patchwork.

Three structural corrections to round 3

  • The flip-cost equivalence is withdrawn, not re-interpreted. The preregistered dynamic-range check: under "perfect switch (0/60 flipped) vs capacity control behaving like no defense (60/60)", round 3's censored-median test returns p = 1.0; the replacement flipped@100 returns p = 2.1·10⁻³⁵. That test was not weak evidence of equivalence — it was no evidence at all.
  • The resistant tail is not routing. The switch arm is bimodal (11/60 resist strongly, AUC < 0.6; SD 0.153 vs 0.060 for corrected, Levene p = 1.3·10⁻⁸) — but the H=4 capacity control has 9/60 strong resisters (Fisher p = 0.81, SD ratio 1.05) while the corrected baseline has 0/60 (p = 5.9·10⁻⁴). The tail is a property of having few live units.
  • Equivalence machinery validated. Positive control (switch vs no-defense, functional) returns REAL DIFFERENCE, d = −0.178, p = 5·10⁻⁵. Truth-exactly-zero calibration (random split-halves, n = 30 v 30) bounds at 0.118 / 0.104, so the C1 bound of 0.065 at n = 60 is genuinely tighter than noise — but 0.05 was out of reach as preregistered (~36 % power).
Untouched: round 3's D1 — benign fine-tuning erases the composite selectability criterion in 100 steps (23/23 → 12/23, p = 3.05·10⁻⁵) — was explicitly out of scope and remains the strongest single argument against the switch as a shipped artifact. Two preregistered budget drops fired (the aligned_chance prereg-attacker cell and the switch_misaligned readout positive control); the readout control is carried over from round 3, not re-run.

Replacement sentence for the write-up: At n = 60 per arm the switch and a routing-free model of the same live parameter count differ under both LoRA attackers, in opposite directions depending on attacker strength; under the realistic functional attacker the difference is bounded at 0.065 AUC (36 % of the total defense effect), and the resistant tail that drives the switch's apparent advantage is present in the capacity control at the same rate (11/60 vs 9/60, p = 0.81).

i) THE PAPER

Exact title

Routing labels a switch that training already built: post-hoc worldview selection in underdetermined models

The round-3 working title (Routing is basis-free; localization is not) is now wrong on both halves — localization is partly solved, and the more interesting fact is that the switch is not created by routing at all. Lead with the latent switch, present routing as a reliability-and-labelling device with a measured +15-point effect, and let the localization result be the technical core rather than the headline.

The 3–5 claims it can defend

C1 — Underdetermined training already builds a latent, selectable worldview switch.
85 % of standard-trained models contain two unit-disjoint halves, each individually competent on the training distribution and committed to the opposite worldview, findable from held-out agreeing data alone: 51/60, replicating 15/20 on independently chosen fresh seeds, against random 8/8 splits 1/50 (p = 1.5·10⁻⁷) and noise-dimension splits 0/10 (p = 7.1·10⁻⁴). The underlying lottery is real at both scales (12 transformer control seeds at held-out 1.000 with deploy alignment 0.000–0.933, χ²(11) = 15213). Underdetermination does not produce an entangled compromise; it produces a switch nobody labelled.
C2 — Gradient routing makes that switch reliable and labelled, at a measured +15 points, and it survives substantial parameter sharing.
60/60 vs a fairly-scored 51/60, +0.150 [+0.058, +0.261], p = 0.0028. It tolerates 25 % and 50 % overlapping unit groups, a fully shared first hidden layer, and a training mask differing from the evaluation mask with 91 % of parameters receiving gradient from both worldviews — all bounded within 11.4 points of the disjoint ceiling. It breaks when private capacity runs out (75 % overlap → 2/60, with 0/58 competence misses). The mechanism is exact: Δ_full = Δ_A + Δ_B + bias (residual 3.8·10⁻⁶), each column a closed-form XOR rule read off the weights (44/44), the un-ablated model a margin race (95.7 %), and the α dial a per-input threshold whose smooth population curve is exactly the CDF of per-input flip points (99.96 % flip once). Scales to k = 4 and to a 68.5k-parameter from-scratch transformer with a shared residual stream.
C3 — Localization has an exact obstruction at cue symmetry, and it is solvable just off it — for orthogonal entanglement.
At exactly equal cue strength the labelled distribution's orthogonal symmetry group has order 64 and its orbit on the true split has two members, so any equivariant localizer must coin-flip; at every other ratio tested |G| = 16 and the orbit is a single point (exact enumeration, 16 ratios). A localizer reading the signed spectrum of D = E[xx′|y=1] − E[xx′|y=0] recovers the true split 40/40 at r = 2.05 and lifts selectability to 38/50 vs 26/50 for ICA on the same seeds (McNemar p = 0.017); the threshold is r* = 1.15 at N = 800 with (r*² − 1) ~ N−1/2. Whitening — the first step of ICA and of most component-analysis pipelines — is precisely what destroys the resource, and re-pairing whitened components does not repair it (18/40): a pure interaction in which neither ingredient helps alone. Under general invertible entanglement the localizer degrades to 13/20 and the claim does not hold.
C4 — The exchange rate says routing buys optionality, not alignment.
Four labelled disagreeing examples — 0.5 % of the training set — buy a working switch in the obstructed basis (localization 25/30, selectability 21/30, both trends p < 5·10⁻⁵), and also resolve which switch position is aligned. The same four examples, simply added to training, align 24/30 models outright; eight align 30/30 while the switch is still at 22/30 (p = 0.0046). Routing never beat augmentation at any dose tested. Agreeing examples buy nothing (8/30 vs 25/30, p = 2·10⁻⁵) — it is disagreement, not data. The one channel routing could uniquely use, unlabelled deployment inputs, saturates at 57 % and does not improve from m = 1 to m = 32. Deferred selection is worth paying for only when the symmetry-breaking information arrives in a form you cannot train on, or when the deployment label is contested — and this paper does not test those settings.
C5 — It is not a safety property, in four preregistered ways.
(i) A masked switch flips at 0 steps, 0 data, 60/60 — structural. (ii) Under the realistic functional attacker the switch is indistinguishable from a routing-free model of the same live-parameter count, bounded at 0.065 AUC = 36 % of the total 0.178 defense effect, and under the strongest honest attacker the capacity control is more resistant (+0.093, p = 0.031); the resistant tail that drives the apparent advantage appears in the capacity control at the same rate (11/60 vs 9/60, p = 0.81) and is absent from the corrected baseline (0/60). (iii) Ordinary benign fine-tuning erases the selectability criterion in 100 steps — 5 % of the training budget, no adversary (23/23 → 12/23, p = 3.05·10⁻⁵) — and nothing maintains the block-diagonal structure once the routing loss is off (cross-block mass 0 → 5.59). (iv) The unselected model is not a hedger (D = 0.786 and 0.951 in two substrates, CIs excluding 0.5) but a confident off-distribution class collapse (majority-class 0.893 vs 0.550, p = 1.3·10⁻¹⁰, at confidence 0.911, while scoring 1.000 on agreeing data), invisible to a per-input confidence monitor (AUROC 0.521, 90 % CI [0.477, 0.549]; the mechanistic readout of the same inputs scores 0.9996; model-level triage is anti-informative at 0.197). Selection is a mandatory deployment step and there is no monitor that catches you skipping it.

What it must concede up front — in the abstract, not a footnote

1. The effect size in our own earlier drafts was wrong. Routing-free floors of 0/30 and 0/20 were produced by scoring the control under an arbitrary fixed unit partition. The fair floor is 85 %, and routing's contribution is +15 points [5.8, 26.1], not +98.
2. Toy scale throughout. ≤11-dimensional synthetic inputs, ≤1.2k-parameter MLPs and one 68.5k-parameter from-scratch transformer, searched-for equal-cost XOR cues. Closed-form column readability is a property of a worldview that is a 2-bit XOR; "154 parameters, and here is the rule" is an existence proof, not evidence that a real worldview module would be readable.
3. Selectability still requires private capacity per worldview, and the sharing-vs-private-width confound is untested (no width sweep at fixed overlap). What we broke is "structurally disjoint subnetworks", not "each worldview needs some of its own units".
4. The oracle-free localizer is a model-based estimator. D's diagonality follows from the generative family; the 40/40 is a numerical confirmation of exact algebra, and it is oracle-free per instance but embodies designer knowledge. It covers orthogonal entanglement only; general invertible entanglement degrades to 13/20 and is the obvious next control.
5. Routing is dominated by data augmentation whenever the symmetry-breaking information is labelled and trainable. The motivation must therefore be an optionality argument — information arriving after training, contested deployment labels, or an unlabelled channel — and none of those settings is tested here.
6. It is not a safety mechanism, and one of our own safety-shaped results (monitor invisibility) is a limit of confidence monitoring in general, not a routing-specific liability. Ordinary underdetermined models are equally invisible.
7. Two families of numbers remain demonstrations, not effect sizes: k-way selection (train mask == eval mask, disjoint columns by construction, and 20/20 conditional on w = 8 — w = 4 gives 13/20 and 6/20), and the transformer's 11/12 (each group trained in isolation under exactly its evaluation mask).

The one sentence to open with

Underdetermined training does not average two incompatible worldviews together — it builds both and picks one by lottery; gradient routing labels the resulting switch and makes it reliable, at a cost of about fifteen points and a localization problem that is provably unsolvable at exactly equal cue strength, solvable just off it, and cheaper to solve with four labelled examples that you would rather have spent on training.
